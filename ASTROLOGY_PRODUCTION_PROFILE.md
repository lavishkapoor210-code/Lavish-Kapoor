## 2. Presenter and look

* **On-camera presenter:** Lavish himself, generated from his own photo
  (image-to-video, first frame). He is the face of every episode so far.
* Wardrobe: navy sherwani with gold embroidery and gold constellation
  pattern, high collar; red tilak; styled hair, full beard and curled
  moustache.
* Background: deep-space galaxy, gold zodiac wheel / astrolabe rings, nebula
  glow. Navy + gold palette.
* Framing: medium close-up, centred, chest-up; steady camera, very slow
  push-in.
* Performance: speaks straight to camera, warm and confident, "sharing a
  secret"; open-palm gestures at chest level, raised finger on numbers,
  folded hands for remedies, smile + nod at the end.
* Generation rules (from the prompts): keep face, beard, hair, clothes and
  background identical to the photo; **no background music, no subtitles, no
  text on screen** in the generated clip (text is added in the edit).

## 3. Script structure (short Reel)

Prompts are written in **English**; dialogue is **Hindi (Devanagari)**,
conversational, second person ("आप"). Five beats, one per clip:

| # | Beat | Moolank 9 example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hook: stop the scroll, name who it is for | "रुकिए! अगर आपका जन्म 9, 18 या 27 तारीख को हुआ है…" |
| 2 | Number + ruling planet + core identity | "मूलांक 9 … ग्रह मंगल — सेनापति" |
| 3 | Personality, then the flaw / warning | "…पर अंदर से सबसे बड़े दिल वाले … जल्दबाज़ी और गुस्सा" |
| 4 | Remedy (उपाय) | "हर मंगलवार हनुमान जी को लाल फूल…" |
| 5 | Lucky number + colour, CTA, follow | "लकी नंबर — 9, लकी रंग — लाल … कैप्शन पढ़िए … फ़ॉलो कीजिए Lavish Astro" |

Each line targets one ~8 s clip. Lines over ~20 words risk being rushed or
cut (beat 3 is at the limit).

## 4. Voice

* **Current:** the presenter's voice is generated by Google Flow (Veo)
  together with the picture, lip-synced. No TTS, no separate narration track.
* Tone: warm, confident Indian male voice, clear Hindi pronunciation.
* **Open decision:** ElevenLabs / Sarvam narration cannot be lip-synced onto
  Flow clips. It fits B-roll scenes (cosmic visuals, symbols) and long-form
  narration, not the talking-head shots. See `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`.

## 5. Edit pattern (as produced)

* Hard cuts between clips, in script order. No transitions, music or SFX.
* Measured: each Flow clip is 10.0 s at 24 fps, 720×1280, H.264 + AAC
  48 kHz stereo; speech ends up to ~1.8 s before the clip ends → the edit
  should trim trailing silence (not done yet).
* Loudness of the joined edit: mean −20.9 dB, peak −1.2 dB (not normalised).
* Output: H.264 CRF 18 + AAC; the source is 720p, so a 1080×1920 export is
  an upscale.

## 10. Tools in use today

Google Flow (manual, in the browser) for clips · Python + Pillow (Raqm) for
Hindi overlays, crops and cover · FFmpeg for joining and encoding · Gemini API
key exists but has **no video quota** (billing off), and Veo is not available
on it through the API.

## 11. Rules derived from this profile

1. The presenter talking head stays the core of short Reels.
2. Prompts in English, dialogue in Hindi, one beat per clip, ≤ ~20 words.
3. No text, music or subtitles baked into generated clips; all text is
   added in the edit with a shaping-aware renderer.
4. Navy + gold cosmic look; gold titles, white body text, red accent.
5. Every Reel has a top title, bottom CTA, logo, matching cover and caption.
6. Never invent dates, transits or predictions; numerology facts come from
   the script.
